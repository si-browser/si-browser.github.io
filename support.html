Frequently Asked Questions

Which version includes WhatsApp Web? ▶
Install SiBrowser from Google Play to use Web Scanner, WhatsApp Web account spaces, local archive tools, and Game Center. The direct SiMontok APK is a separate browser-only edition and does not include these features.
How do I link a WhatsApp Web account? ▶
Open Web Scanner, choose an account space, and use the QR code or phone-number option provided by WhatsApp Web. Complete the linking steps in WhatsApp on your primary phone.
How many WhatsApp Web accounts can I use? ▶
Two isolated account spaces are included. Web Scanner Pro unlocks account spaces 3–5, for a total of five separate sign-ins.
Can SiBrowser recover deleted WhatsApp messages? ▶
No. After you grant notification access, the Pro archive can save text from future WhatsApp notifications that reach your device. It cannot recover content from a server, content deleted before access was granted, or messages that did not produce a notification.
How do media archive and Status Saver work? ▶
Select the WhatsApp Media folder through Android's system file picker. SiBrowser can then show media and status files already available in that folder. You can preview, save, share, or locally archive selected files. Folder access can be revoked in Android settings.
Can I share webpages and saved files? ▶
Yes. Use Android's share panel to share the current webpage, a downloaded file, or selected archived media. SiBrowser shares only the item you choose with the destination app you select.
Does SiBrowser upload my WhatsApp messages or media? ▶
SiBrowser keeps its message and media archive copies on your device by default. WhatsApp Web itself connects to WhatsApp's service and is governed by WhatsApp's policies. See our Privacy Policy for complete details.
How do I update SiBrowser? ▶
Google Play manages updates for the SiBrowser edition. The separate direct-download SiMontok edition checks the official update manifest and downloads its latest APK from our Cloudflare-hosted download service.
