Frequently Asked Questions

How do I install Si Browser? ▶
You can install Si Browser from Google Play, APKPure, or by downloading the APK directly from GitHub Releases. If installing from APK, enable "Install from unknown sources" in your Android settings.
How does the video detection work? ▶
Si Browser automatically detects video URLs on webpages using a built-in browser extension. When a video is found, a floating button appears. Tap it to open the video list, then tap Play to launch the native full-screen player.
Why can't I play some videos? ▶
Some videos are protected by DRM (Digital Rights Management) or require specific authentication tokens. These videos may not be playable in the native player. Try watching them directly in the browser instead.
How do I install Firefox extensions? ▶
Go to the browser menu → Add-ons → tap the settings icon → Enable "Install extensions from unknown sources". You can then install any Firefox-compatible extension from addons.mozilla.org.
Is the ad blocker enabled by default? ▶
Yes, uBlock Origin is pre-installed and active by default. You can manage its settings from the Extensions menu in the browser.
Does Si Browser collect my data? ▶
No. Si Browser does not collect telemetry, browsing history, or any personal data. All your data stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
How do I report a bug? ▶
You can report bugs by emailing us at applogcat@gmail.com or by opening an issue on our GitHub repository. Please include your Android version, device model, and steps to reproduce the issue.
How do I update Si Browser? ▶
If installed from Google Play, updates are delivered automatically. If installed via APK, download the latest version from our GitHub Releases page and install it over the existing app.
